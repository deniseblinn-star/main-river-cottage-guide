# Main River Cottage Planner v3.5.0 — Phase 6 Activities

## Added
- Reusable Activity Catalog with Create, Edit, Copy and Delete.
- Date-specific Activity scheduling inside the active Overall Event.
- Activity participants, external guests, host, location, times and notes.
- Date-specific Linked Meal Slots.
- Linked meals display their current recipes, restaurant or simple food plan.
- Template equipment, checklist and music.
- Checklist completion is specific to each scheduled activity.
- Existing Main River activities migrate into the new model.

## Architecture
Activities link to Meal Slots. Meal Slots own Recipes. Recipes drive Groceries.

## Deferred
- Activity photo albums.
- Equipment inventory.
- Grocery Item Library and Units Engine.
